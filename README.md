# sikitsikit.my
AI Chatbot Portfolio for Malaysian Insurance
Sikitsikit.my - localized AI Insurtech Platform 🇲🇾

Sikitsikit.my is a concept Insurtech platform designed specifically for the Malaysian market. It bridges the gap between complex insurance policies and potential policyholders using a hyper-localized AI assistant.

🔴 Live Demo: Click here to view the live site

🤖 Meet "CikBijakSikit" (The AI Persona)

The core of this project is CikBijakSikit (Miss Slightly-Smart), an embedded AI chatbot engineered to feel like a helpful friend rather than a corporate robot.

🌟 Key Differentiator: Hyper-Localization

Unlike standard chatbots that speak robotic English, CikBijakSikit is trained to understand the Malaysian Context:

Dual Language Support: Fluent in both Bahasa Melayu and English.

"Manglish" Capable: Understands mixed-language queries commonly used by Malaysians (e.g., "Medical card covers what ah?", "Ada waiting period tak?").

Jargon-Free Zone: Explains complex terms like "Deductible" or "Co-takaful" using simple analogies (e.g., comparing premiums to paying for a monthly streaming subscription).

AI Persona Prompt Strategy

I designed the system prompt to ensure the bot remains humble and approachable:

"You are CikBijakSikit. You are not a know-it-all expert. You are a helpful companion who knows just enough to keep the user safe. Always guide them gently and avoid sounding intimidating."

📋 The Problem & Solution

The Problem

Information Overload: Malaysians often find insurance policies confusing and intimidating.

Time Constraints: Agents aren't available 24/7 to answer simple pre-sales questions.

Trust Barrier: Users are hesitant to approach agents directly for fear of aggressive hard-selling.

The Solution

A web-based "warm-up" funnel:

Educate: Users chat anonymously with CikBijakSikit to get 24/7 answers.

Pre-qualify: The AI assesses the user's needs naturally during the conversation.

Convert: Once the user feels confident, the AI funnels them to a subscription plan or human agent.
